I'm building [StillHere](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/stillhere), my flagship tool for brain-imaging researchers planning studies where people listen to stories.

> **Pre-registered result · 22 listeners**
>
> TRIBE v2's language readout was stronger for an intact story than for its sentence-shuffled version: **p = 0.00516**.
>
> [Result](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/stillhere/blob/main/results/d20_notthefall.json) · [Protocol](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/stillhere/blob/main/PREREG.md) · [Scoring hash registry](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/stillhere/blob/main/prereg_registry.json)

The [failed secondary test](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/stillhere/blob/main/results/d20_notthefall.json) and [order-map no-go](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/stillhere/blob/main/results/order_map_gonogo.json) remain published alongside the positive result.

I also build [Fractured Perception](https://github.com/Yatsuiii/fractured_perception/tree/claude/gameplay-itt), a cooperative Unity game.
